BEFORE WE TALK

A few useful answers.

Is Meld an AI SRE or an incident response platform?

Meld is an AI-native incident response platform with its own investigator. We’re building it to carry incidents through diagnosis, fixes, deployment and verified recovery. Engineers and their agents can shape the same investigation, and the record carries through to postmortems and follow-ups.

Does my whole team need to join every incident?

No. Meld can investigate without everyone joining. Responders come in when they can help, with the evidence and open questions already available. Participation should move the incident forward, not create another meeting.

Who controls access and changes to production?

Your team sets the boundaries. Meld’s approach is scoped authority: the resources an agent can access, the actions it can take, and the approvals a change requires. Each action should be attributable to the agent and the responder who authorized it.

How do we get started?

We’re looking for our first design partners. Start with a 20-minute demo and a conversation about a recent incident. We’ll agree on the services, integrations and access needed before connecting your environment.

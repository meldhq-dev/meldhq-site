Privacy note

Updated October 3, 2026

This note covers the early-access request form on meldhq.net.

What we collect

When you submit the form, it sends your work email address to app.meldhq.net. We store the address, request status, and review timestamps to manage invitations. Submitting the form does not create a MeldHQ account.

How we use it

MeldHQ owners review requests and may contact you about early access. An approved request still requires a separate sign-in and invitation process.

Questions or removal

To ask about your request or have your email removed from the early-access list, write to sricharanravi@meldhq.net.

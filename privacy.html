WEBSITE PRIVACY

Your choice.

Updated October 17, 2026. This note covers meldhq.net and the contact form on this website.

Contact and early-access requests

When you submit your work email, the form sends it to app.meldhq.net. We store your address, request status and review timestamps so MeldHQ’s founders can respond and manage invitations. Submitting the form does not create a product account. Your email and form contents are not sent to website analytics.

Optional website analytics

With your permission, we use PostHog Cloud in the United States to understand page visits, referral domains, campaign labels, approximate location, browser and device types, and interactions such as demo clicks, video progress and successful contact requests. PostHog processes network information, including your IP address, to provide these reports. We do not use this to identify anonymous visitors by name.

Analytics loads only after you choose “Allow analytics.” It uses browser storage to recognize visits. We disable session recording, automatic capture of clicks and form contents, and person profiles. We remove URL queries, fragments and full referrer paths from events, apart from limited campaign labels. We do not send your incident data or product credentials to this website analytics project.

You can change your choice below or from the footer. Declining stops analytics collection; it does not affect access to the website. We also respect Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals. Your choice is saved in your browser. Withdrawing permission stops future collection and clears the tracker’s local stored data; it does not retroactively remove events already received by PostHog.

Read PostHog’s privacy policy for how the provider processes data.

Booking a demo

Our booking links open Google Calendar. Google handles the booking information under its own terms and privacy policy. A click on a booking link is measured as interest, not a confirmed meeting.

Questions or removal

To ask about your information or have your email removed from our contact list, write to sricharanravi@meldhq.net.
